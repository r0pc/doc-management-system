ACME CORPORATION - INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Department: Information Security & Compliance
Date: August 2026

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines the baseline operational security practices for all internal personnel.
All staff members must adhere to access control protocols and protect company assets.

2. Access Control
Access to systems and document repositories is granted according to role and department clearance.
Sharing credentials is strictly prohibited under disciplinary penalty.

3. Document Handling
Documents containing general internal workflows must be stored in secure document repositories.
